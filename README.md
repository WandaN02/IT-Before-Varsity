# IT Before Varsity

**Preparing South African learners for the digital world, one lesson at a time.**

## About the Project

IT Before Varsity is an educational web platform designed to help South African Grade 8–12 learners develop essential computer literacy and beginner programming skills before entering university, TVET colleges or other tertiary institutions.

The platform aims to support learners, particularly those whose schools do not offer Information Technology (IT) or Computer Applications Technology (CAT).

Its goal is to make technology education more accessible through structured lessons, practical activities, assessments and personalised learning support.

## Problem Statement

Some South African learners enter tertiary education without sufficient computer skills because they have limited exposure to computer studies at school.

This can make it difficult for them to adapt to technology-related modules at university and TVET colleges.

IT Before Varsity aims to help bridge this gap by providing accessible computer education and practical learning opportunities.

## Target Audience

- South African Grade 8–12 learners.
- Learners whose schools do not offer IT or CAT.
- Learners preparing for university or TVET college.
- Beginners interested in developing computer and programming skills.

## Planned Learning Areas

The initial learning areas will include:

- **Microsoft Word:** Document creation and formatting.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Spreadsheets, formulas and data management.
- **Microsoft PowerPoint:** Creating presentations.
- **HTML:** Introduction to web development.

## Planned Features

### 1. Learner Dashboard
- Personal learner accounts.
- Access to learning materials.
- Subject progress tracking.
- Assessment results.
- Learning recommendations.

### 2. Teacher Dashboard
- Teacher registration and verification.
- Publishing and managing lessons.
- Uploading teaching videos.
- Providing learner support.

### 3. Practical Learning and Assessments
- Written notes and video lessons.
- Practical assignments.
- Interactive exercises.
- Automatic marking of supported practical assignments.
- Unlimited assessment attempts.
- Minimum passing mark of 50%.
- Feedback to help learners understand their mistakes.

### 4. Kiki – AI Learning Assistant

Kiki will be the platform's friendly AI learning companion.

Planned capabilities include:
- Explaining difficult concepts in simple language.
- Answering learner questions.
- Providing practical examples.
- Guiding learners through assignments.
- Explaining assessment mistakes.
- Recommending lessons based on learner progress.
- Supporting English and South African languages.
- Voice and text interaction.

### 5. Questions and Answers
Learners will be able to receive assistance from teachers, other learners and Kiki.

### 6. Additional Learning Tools
- Online scam checker.
- Tertiary readiness quiz.
- Learner progress reports.

## User Roles

The platform will have three main user roles:

- **Administrator:** Manages the platform, users and learning content.
- **Teacher:** Creates lessons, uploads videos and supports learners.
- **Learner:** Accesses lessons, completes activities and tracks progress.
